MMModern Linux test bundle - tester's guide
==========================================

MMModern is an open reimplementation of the engine of Might and Magic IV
and V (World of Xeen). This bundle is a test build of the native Linux
version, not a release. SOURCE.txt names the commit it was built from.
Thank you for checking the few things that only a real Linux desktop shows:
sound, CD speech, and how keyboard, mouse and the window feel.

What you need
-------------

- 64-bit x86 Linux with a desktop (X11 or Wayland) and sound.
- Your own English World of Xeen CD edition, installed in a folder. GOG's
  "Might and Magic 4-5" works: it is the folder with WORLD/XEEN.DAT,
  GAME1.INS and MUSIC/. The bundle holds no game files, and MMModern never
  changes yours. Sound devices come from the installed WORLD/XEEN.CFG.

Start
-----

    tar -xzf mmodern-linux-test-*.tar.gz
    cd mmodern-linux-test-*
    ./mmodern.sh "$HOME/Games/Might and Magic 4-5"

Use your own game folder. If the game does not start, run
./mmodern.sh --diagnostics "<game folder>" and send its output.

Saves, settings, window.ini and the log are in ~/.local/share/MMModern
(or $XDG_DATA_HOME/MMModern). Saves on a network drive are refused; set
XDG_DATA_HOME to a local folder in that case.

Keys: arrows move and turn, Space talks or continues, Tab opens the panel
(Save, Save As, Load, Quit), Escape goes back, Alt+Enter switches between
window and full screen. All keys:
https://github.com/carbiecarbie/MMModern/blob/main/docs/controls.md

What to check (about 15 minutes)
--------------------------------

1. Title, intro sound and music. The first start plays the Darkside intro
   with its sound and music, then the title screen with its music. Is the
   sound clean, without crackles, gaps or wrong speed?
2. New game. On the title choose New, a slot, a name and Adventurer. The
   party stands in Vertigo at (18,4) facing West (M shows the map,
   coordinates and facing).
3. CD speech. Walk to (14,5) and turn to face West: Mayor Gunther talks
   (press Space if he does not). His words are spoken from the CD audio
   while his face moves. Is the voice clear and in step with the face?
4. Keyboard and mouse. Walk around Vertigo, click the buttons and the
   portraits. Do keys and clicks respond at once, without lost or doubled
   input? Resize the window, maximize it, and press Alt+Enter twice. Does
   the picture stay sharp, in the right shape, with the mouse on target?
5. Save and reload. Tab, Save As, pick a slot. Tab, Quit. Start again,
   choose Load on the title and that slot. The party is where you saved.

What to send back
-----------------

For each item: OK, or what went wrong. Also your distribution and version,
X11 or Wayland, PipeWire or PulseAudio, and ~/.local/share/MMModern/logs/
mmodern.log (and mmodern-previous.log after a crash). Never send game files.
